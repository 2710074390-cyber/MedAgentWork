# Humanize Prose — 去 AI 文风

让文本像人写的，不是 AI 生成的。

## Core Rules

### 1. Cut filler phrases

Remove throat-clearing openers ("In today's digital age", "It is worth noting that", "When it comes to"), emphasis crutches ("importantly", "significantly", "essentially"), and all adverbs that don't do real work. See [references/phrases.md](references/phrases.md).

### 2. Break formulaic structures

Avoid:
- Binary contrasts ("It's not X, it's Y") — state Y directly
- Negative listings ("Not this, not that, not the other thing") — say what it is
- Dramatic fragmentation — one-sentence paragraphs for "impact"
- Rhetorical setups ("Here's the thing:", "The truth is:")
- False agency ("The data suggests", "The research shows") — who did the research?
- Meta-joiners ("The rest of this essay...") — let the prose move

See [references/structures.md](references/structures.md).

### 3. Use active voice

Every sentence needs a human subject doing something. No passive constructions ("it was decided" → "we decided"). No inanimate objects performing human actions ("the complaint becomes a fix" → name the person who fixed it).

### 4. Be specific

No vague declaratives ("The implications are significant" — name the specific implication). No lazy extremes ("every", "always", "never") doing vague work. Replace abstractions with concrete details.

### 5. Put the reader in the room

No narrator-from-a-distance voice. "You" beats "People." Specifics beat abstractions. Second person and first person are your friends.

### 6. Vary rhythm

Mix sentence lengths. Two items beats three (the rule of three is an AI tell). End paragraphs differently — not always with a punchy one-liner. No em dashes used as a crutch.

### 7. Trust readers

State facts directly. Skip softening ("might", "perhaps", "could be considered"), justification ("This is important because..."), and hand-holding ("Let's take a closer look at...").

### 8. Cut quotables

If a sentence sounds like it could be a pull-quote or motivational poster, rewrite it. Real humans don't write in aphorisms.

## Quick Checks

Before delivering prose, check:

- Any adverbs? Kill them.
- Any passive voice? Find the actor, make them the subject.
- Inanimate thing doing a human verb ("the decision emerges")? Name the person.
- Sentence starts with a Wh- word ("What", "Why", "How")? Restructure it.
- Any "here's what/this/that" throat-clearing? Cut to the point.
- Any "not X, it's Y" contrasts? State Y directly.
- Three consecutive sentences match length? Break one.
- Paragraph ends with punchy one-liner? Vary it.
- Em-dash anywhere? Remove it.
- Vague declarative ("The implications are significant")? Name the specific implication.
- Narrator-from-a-distance ("Nobody designed this")? Put the reader in the scene.
- Meta-joiners ("The rest of this essay...")? Delete. Let the essay move.

## Scoring

Rate 1-10 on each dimension:

| Dimension | Question |
|-----------|---------|
| Directness | Statements or announcements? |
| Rhythm | Varied or metronomic? |
| Trust | Respects reader intelligence? |
| Authenticity | Sounds human? |
| Density | Anything cuttable? |

**Below 35/50: revise.**
